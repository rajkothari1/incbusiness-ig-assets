# Info Edge Business Model Explained

Info Edge started in the servants' quarters of its founder's father's house. Today it runs Naukri.com, 99acres, Jeevansathi and Shiksha, and earned ₹3,052 crore in FY26.

This Info Edge case study covers the Naukri business model, Info Edge revenue by business, and its product and marketing strategy.

**The model in one line:** Info Edge runs online classifieds where users browse for free and businesses pay to reach them.

Info Edge runs four online classifieds where users browse for free and businesses pay. **Naukri brings in about 74% of revenue at a 57% operating margin**, while 99acres, Jeevansathi and Shiksha are much smaller. Its newest focus is AI, with about ₹150 crore of AI spending planned for FY27.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Info Edge's business model?**
Info Edge runs online classifieds where businesses pay for access: Naukri for jobs, 99acres for property, Jeevansathi for matrimony and Shiksha for education.

**Who owns Naukri.com?**
Naukri.com is owned by Info Edge (India) Ltd, founded by Sanjeev Bikhchandani. Naukri launched in 1997.

**What is Info Edge's revenue in FY26?**
₹3,052 crore on a standalone basis, up 15% from FY25. Consolidated revenue was about ₹3,285 crore.

**How does Naukri make money?**
Mainly from companies and recruiters paying for job postings and access to its resume database. Job seekers can also pay for services like resume writing and profile boosts.

**Is Info Edge profitable?**
Yes. Its standalone operating profit was ₹1,138 crore in FY26, a 37% operating margin.

**What is JobHai?**
JobHai is Info Edge's free jobs app for lower salary jobs. It earns from paid extras on top of free use.

**How does 99acres make money?**
99acres earns from builders, brokers and property owners who pay to list and advertise properties. It made ₹488 crore in revenue in FY26.

**Which is Info Edge's most profitable business?**
Recruitment (Naukri). It made ₹1,277 crore in operating profit in FY26 at a 57% margin, while 99acres and Jeevansathi were still making losses.

**When did Info Edge go public?**
Info Edge listed on the stock market in 2006, the first internet company in India to do so. Its NSE symbol is NAUKRI.

**Who are Info Edge's competitors?**
LinkedIn, Indeed and foundit in jobs; MagicBricks, Housing.com and NoBroker in property; and Shaadi.com and BharatMatrimony in matrimony.
