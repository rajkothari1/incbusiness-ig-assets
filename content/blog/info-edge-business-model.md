# Info Edge Business Model Explained

Info Edge started in the servants' quarters of its founder's father's house. Today it runs Naukri.com, earned ₹3,052 crore in FY26, and owns some of India's most valuable startup stakes.

This Info Edge case study covers the Naukri business model, Info Edge revenue by business and its investment portfolio.

**The model in one line:** Info Edge runs online classifieds that companies and people pay to use, and invests the cash in young startups.

## The Problem Info Edge Solved

1. **Jobs were hidden in newspapers.** In the 1990s, people in India found jobs through newspaper ads and had to check many papers.
2. **Companies struggled to find people.** Recruiters had no easy way to reach good candidates.
3. **The answer: Naukri.com.** In 1996, Sanjeev Bikhchandani saw a stall marked "WWW" at an IT fair and decided to put jobs online. Naukri.com launched in 1997.
4. **The same idea, new markets.** Info Edge later did the same for property (99acres), marriage (Jeevansathi) and colleges (Shiksha).

## Info Edge Target Audience

- **Companies and recruiters** who pay to post jobs and search resumes
- **Job seekers**, who use Naukri for free; some pay for resume help and visibility
- **Builders, brokers and property owners** who pay to list on 99acres
- **Families and individuals** looking for a life partner on Jeevansathi
- **Colleges and universities** that pay Shiksha to reach students

## Core Pillars of the Info Edge Business Model

### 1. Online Classifieds Where Businesses Pay
- **What:** Info Edge runs marketplaces that connect two sides: recruiters and job seekers, sellers and buyers, colleges and students.
- **How it works:** Most users browse for free. The side with money pays: recruiters pay to post jobs and search resumes, brokers and builders pay to list property, colleges pay to reach students.
- **Why it matters:** Once a platform has the most users, it attracts the most paying customers, and that keeps users coming back.

### 2. Naukri: The Cash Machine
- **What:** Naukri is Info Edge's job platform, with about 115 million resumes and ~21,000 new resumes added every day (Q4 FY26).
- **How it works:** Companies buy subscriptions for job postings and resume database access. Job seekers can also pay for services like resume writing and profile boosts.
- **Why it matters:** Recruitment brings in ~74% of Info Edge's revenue at a 57% operating margin. This cash funds every other business and investment.

### 3. Customers Pay Upfront
- **What:** Info Edge collects money before it delivers the full service.
- **How it works:** Recruiters and advertisers pay for subscriptions in advance. In FY26, Info Edge billed ₹3,178 crore but booked ₹3,052 crore as revenue; the rest is booked as the service is delivered.
- **Why it matters:** The business generates more cash than profit, which is why it has ₹4,963 crore in the bank.

### 4. Investing in Startups
- **What:** Info Edge uses its own cash, and money from outside investors in funds it manages, to back young startups.
- **How it works:** It invests early and stays invested for years. Its first big bets were Zomato (now Eternal) and Policybazaar.
- **Why it matters:** About ₹4,900 crore invested in 135 startups is now valued at about ₹41,300 crore (8.4x).

### 5. AI Built Into the Products
- **What:** Info Edge is adding AI across its platforms, like AI-Rex, an AI assistant for recruiters on Naukri.
- **How it works:** AI helps match candidates to jobs, automate recruiter work and improve recommendations.
- **Why it matters:** Management sees AI as strengthening its platforms, not replacing them, because it owns the data and the user base.

## How Info Edge Makes Money

Here's how Naukri makes money, and the rest of Info Edge too. Info Edge earns from **four online businesses** (FY26, standalone):

| Business | What it earns from | FY26 revenue | Share | Profit / loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Naukri (recruitment)** | Recruiter subscriptions, resume database access, services for job seekers, NaukriGulf | ₹2,256 Cr | ~74% | ₹1,277 Cr profit (57% margin) |
| **99acres (real estate)** | Listings and ads from builders, brokers and owners | ₹488 Cr | ~16% | ₹59 Cr loss |
| **Shiksha (education)** | Colleges and universities paying to reach students | ₹170 Cr | ~6% | ₹13 Cr profit |
| **Jeevansathi (matrimony)** | Paid memberships | ₹138 Cr | ~5% | ₹4 Cr loss |
| **Total** | | **₹3,052 Cr** | | **₹1,138 Cr operating profit** |

## Conclusion

Info Edge's playbook: **build the leading online classified, let businesses pay for access, and invest the cash in the next generation of startups.** Naukri pays the bills; Zomato and Policybazaar show what the investment arm can do. That makes Info Edge both a profitable internet company and one of India's most successful startup investors.
