## Slide 2 — The problem they solved

- 2014: Varun & Ghazal Alagh's son was born
- Most baby products in India had harsh chemicals; parents who could afford it brought them back from abroad, which was expensive and slow
- 2016: they launched Mamaearth with **6 toxin-free baby products**
- First brand in Asia with **MADE SAFE** certification

Line: *They didn't start with a factory. They started with a problem they faced at home.*

---

## Slide 3 — The business model

**Digital-first, asset-light FMCG + "house of brands"**

Flow diagram:
`Spot a trend → Contract manufacturer makes it → Sell online (own app, Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, quick commerce) + offline (shops) → Influencers & ads drive demand`

Brands under Honasa:
Mamaearth · The Derma Co · Aqualogica · Dr. Sheth's · BBlunt · Staze · Reginald Men

Line: *Own the brand and the customer. Rent the factory.*

---

## Slide 4 — How they make money

- Sells beauty & personal care products at premium-but-accessible prices
- Revenue comes from online marketplaces + its own D2C site + offline retail (general trade & modern trade)
- **Biggest cost = marketing.** Ad spend was ~33% of revenue in Q4 FY26 (₹216 Cr on ₹657 Cr)
- Growth engine now = younger brands (The Derma Co, Aqualogica, etc.), **growing 40%+ YoY in FY26**, while Mamaearth grows in the teens

---

## Slide 5 — The crash & the comeback

- Nov 2023: IPO at ₹324/share
- Q2 FY25 (Nov 2024): **first quarterly loss (~₹18.6 Cr)**. Distributors were sitting on too much stock
- Stock fell 20% in one day, below the IPO price
- Fix → **"Project Neev":** removed super-stockists, moved to direct distributors in top 50 cities, cleaned up inventory
- FY26: profit back, record quarter, **first-ever dividend (₹3/share)**

---

## Slide 6 — The numbers + lesson

| FY26 | |
|---|---|
| Revenue | **₹2,392 Cr** (+16% YoY) |
| Net profit | **₹200 Cr** (vs ₹73 Cr in FY25, ~2.75x) |
| EBITDA | ₹231 Cr (9.7% margin) |
| Dividend | ₹3/share (first ever) |

**Lesson for founders:**
Selling online and marketing through influencers can take a brand to a few hundred crore.
Getting to thousands of crore needs offline distribution and more than one brand.
