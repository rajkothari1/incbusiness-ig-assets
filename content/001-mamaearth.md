## Slide 1 — Hook

**Two parents couldn't find a safe baby lotion in India.**
**So they built a ₹2,392 Cr company.**

Sub: How Mamaearth makes money 👉

## Slide 2 — The problem they solved

- 2014: Varun & Ghazal Alagh's son Agastya was born
- They couldn't find toxin-free baby products in India, so family and friends brought them from the US
- 2016: they launched Mamaearth with **6 toxin-free baby products**
- Became **Asia's first MADE SAFE-certified brand**

## Slide 3 — The business model

**Digital-first FMCG that doesn't make its own products**

- Honasa owns the brand, the formulas and the customer
- **Third-party contract manufacturers** make the products
- Sells online + offline (marketplaces, quick commerce, its own site, and shops)
- A **house of brands**: Mamaearth · The Derma Co · Aqualogica · Dr. Sheth's · BBlunt · Staze

## Slide 4 — How they make money

- Sells beauty & personal care products, online + in shops
- **Biggest cost = advertising: 32.9% of revenue** in Q4 FY26
- Growth engine now = the younger brands (The Derma Co, Aqualogica, Dr. Sheth's…), **growing 40%+ YoY in FY26**

## Slide 5 — The crash & the comeback

- Nov 2023: IPO at **₹324/share**
- Nov 2024 (Q2 FY25): **first quarterly loss, ~₹18.6 Cr**. Distributors were holding too much stock
- Stock **fell 20% in one day**, below the IPO price
- Fix: **"Project Neev"** removed super-stockists and moved to direct distributors in the top 50 cities

## Slide 6 — The numbers (FY26)

| | |
|---|---|
| Revenue | **₹2,392 Cr** (+16% YoY) |
| Net profit | **₹200 Cr** (FY25: ₹73 Cr) |
| Dividend | **₹3/share**, first ever |
