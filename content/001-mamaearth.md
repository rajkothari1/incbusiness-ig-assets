# #001 — Mamaearth (Honasa Consumer Ltd)

**Series:** 100 Indian Business Models

## Slide 1 — Cover

**How Mamaearth makes ₹2,392 Cr a year**

A digital-first, asset-light D2C + omnichannel FMCG brand, owned by **Honasa Consumer Ltd**.

Business model breakdown 👉

## Slide 2 — The problem it solved

- **2014:** Varun & Ghazal Alagh's son was born. They couldn't find **toxin-free baby products in India**, so family and friends brought them from the US
- **2016:** they launched Mamaearth with **6 toxin-free baby products**, funded with ₹30 lakh of their own savings + ₹60 lakh from friends & family
- **2017:** parents asked "what about us?", so Mamaearth moved into **adult skin & hair care**

## Slide 3 — Core model ①: Asset-light manufacturing

**It owns the formula, not the factory.**

- Products are **formulated in-house**, by an innovation team of ~50 people led by co-founder Ghazal Alagh
- **Third-party contract manufacturers** do the actual production
- In 2024 it bought **Cosmogenesis's R&D lab** (4,000+ formulations, plus a small facility) to speed up product development

Why it matters: less money is locked in factories, so more can go into brands and marketing.

## Slide 4 — Core model ②: Omnichannel distribution

**Started online. Now it's everywhere.**

- **Online:** its own website & app, Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, FirstCry, and quick commerce (Zepto, Instamart)
- **Offline:** offline went from **9% of revenue (FY20) to 29% (FY22)**
- Today it reaches **~3 lakh retail outlets** (June 2026)
- **2024 fix, "Project Neev":** removed the super-stockist middle layer and appointed direct distributors in the top 50 cities

## Slide 5 — Core model ③: House of brands

**One company, many brands, each for a different customer:**

| Brand | Target |
|---|---|
| Mamaearth | Toxin-free, natural personal care for families |
| The Derma Co | Active-ingredient, science-led skincare |
| Aqualogica | Hydration-focused skincare for young users |
| Dr. Sheth's | Skincare for Indian skin |
| BBlunt | Salon-style hair care & styling |
| Staze | Makeup |

- **BBlunt & Dr. Sheth's were acquired in 2022**
- The younger brands **grew 40%+ in FY26**, faster than Mamaearth itself

## Slide 6 — Value proposition & product strategy

**① Toxin-free USP**
- Asia's first **MADE SAFE-certified** brand
- Products are marketed as free from **parabens, sulphates & SLS** (claims vary by product)

**② Data-driven launches**
- Launched **122 new products in 2023**
- Recently launched products = **~9% of revenue** (Q1 FY25)

Spot a trend (onion, rice water, vitamin C) → launch fast → scale what works.

## Slide 7 — Revenue streams & margins

**Revenue = selling products** (skin, hair, baby & personal care) online and offline.

- **Gross margin ~70%** (Q4 FY26). It costs about ₹30 to make and ship ₹100 of products
- But **₹33 of every ₹100 goes to advertising** (32.9%, Q4 FY26)
- Result: net profit of **₹200 Cr on ₹2,392 Cr revenue** in FY26 (~8% net margin)

*High margins on the product. Heavy spending to keep demand coming.*

## Slide 8 — Marketing & growth engine

**① Mom bloggers first.** Before the Instagram era, Ghazal made friends with mom bloggers.
> "When a mother tells another mother… that has the most trust."

**② Free samples instead of ads.** "The cheapest way to market a product is to make our consumers experience the product."

**③ Celebrities for mass reach.** Shilpa Shetty (ambassador + investor, 2018); Sara Ali Khan (face of the Onion hair-care range, 2021)

**④ Cause marketing: #PlantGoodness.** One tree planted for every order on its website & app. **8 lakh+ trees planted**

## Slide 9 — The numbers (FY26)

| | |
|---|---|
| Revenue | **₹2,392 Cr** (+16%) |
| Net profit | **₹200 Cr** (FY25: ₹73 Cr, ~2.75x) |
| Gross margin | **~70%** |
| Ad spend | **32.9% of revenue** (Q4) |
| Retail reach | **~3 lakh outlets** |
| Dividend | **₹3/share**, first ever |
