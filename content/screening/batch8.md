## Notes
- **Small caps (mcap under about ₹30,000 Cr):** RailTel, IIFL Finance, Equitas, Ujjivan, Fino, Aptus, Home First, CreditAccess, Spandana, Five-Star, Metropolis, Thyrocare, Vijaya, Krsnaa, Yatharth, Shalby, Eris, Jubilant Pharmova, Century Ply, Greenply, Prince Pipes, Somany, CDSL, Angel One, KPIT, Bata, Cera, Hatsun, Metro Brands. Anand Rathi Wealth (35,860), Blue Star (32,232) and Voltas (34,051) sit just above the line.
- **Loss-making:** only Spandana Sphoorty (FY26 loss of ₹699 Cr).
- **Tata Motors:** after the Oct 2025 demerger, the listed passenger-vehicle and JLR entity trades as TMPV. Its FY26 profit is dominated by a one-off gain, so do not quote ₹82,645 Cr as operating profit. The CV business is listed separately.
- **Varun Beverages:** reports on a calendar year, so CY2025 is its latest full year.
- **Nestle India:** Screener and StockAnalysis both show a 12-month FY26 (Apr–Mar).
- **Ranges:** where two sources differ slightly, both figures are shown. The gap is usually profit including minority interest versus profit attributable to owners.
- **Web search limit:** the shared web search budget ran out partway through. Rows 12–48 are therefore checked against two data aggregators (Screener and StockAnalysis), not a news article or filing. Rows 1–11 use a news report or company/NSE filing plus Screener.
