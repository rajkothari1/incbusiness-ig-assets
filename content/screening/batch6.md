## Notes and caveats
- **Butterfly, Stove Kraft:** these are standalone figures; I didn't find consolidated ones. One source (5paisa) says Butterfly's PAT is ₹47 Cr.
- **Bata India:** only the standalone FY26 figure (₹328.4 Cr) was found. I didn't find the consolidated full-year number. Q4 consolidated profit fell to ₹2.2 Cr because of one-off VRS and forex costs.
- **Symphony:** the loss of about ₹141 Cr (consolidated) came mainly from exceptional impairments. One aggregator says the loss was ₹166 Cr, possibly on a different basis.
- **Bajaj Electricals:** FY26 net loss of about ₹91 Cr (₹90.86 Cr).
- **Arvind Fashions:** total PAT in the audited extract is ₹183.66 Cr. The ScanX write-up says ₹124 Cr, probably the share belonging to owners after minority interest. The source has not been checked to confirm this.
- **Kewal Kiran:** company-reported PAT is ₹152.3 Cr. Arihant/Capital Market gives ₹141.9 Cr, which is likely the owners' share.
- **Ethos:** Arihant gives ₹94.77 Cr and 5paisa gives ₹96.14 Cr; both agree it was profitable.
- **Thangamayil:** the full-year ₹352 Cr appears in only one article (AskFuzz, citing BusinessLine). Check it against the exchange filing before using it.
- **Cello, La Opala, Go Fashion, Raymond Lifestyle:** the full-year figure was confirmed by only one outlet (ScanX), but the quarterly figures from other outlets are consistent with it.
- **Raymond Lifestyle:** Q4 FY26 was a loss of ₹52 Cr because of exceptional items, but the full year was profitable. Don't confuse it with Raymond Ltd.
- **Borosil Ltd** (consumer glassware) is a different company from Borosil Renewables.
- **Category judgement calls:** Bata, Go Colors and Ethos are G because they sell mostly through their own stores. Vaibhav Global is G because it is a TV and online retailer that holds its own stock. Arvind Fashions is F because its revenue mostly comes from brands it licenses.
