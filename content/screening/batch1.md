## Notes
- Delhivery: UNCLEAR. The Inc42 page (as fetched) says "PAT rose 8% YoY to ₹321 Cr from ₹162 Cr", which does not add up, because 162 to 321 would be a 98% rise. Copy-sites say ₹153 Cr. That matches the quarterly sum (Q1 ~91, Q2 ~-50, Q3 39.6, Q4 72.4). Check the NSE filing before using any figure.
- MakeMyTrip reports in USD (US-listed). The ₹ figure is only a conversion.
- OYO's ₹994 Cr includes a one-time deferred-tax credit of about ₹678 Cr.
- Flipkart: -8,468 is the group (incl. Myntra, Cleartrip). The marketplace entity Flipkart Internet is reported separately.
- Zepto FY26 comes from its updated DRHP, not an RoC filing.
- The web-search budget ran out before checking FY26 for BigBasket, Ola and CRED. Rapido FY26 was searched but not found.
- Category judgment calls: Nykaa, FirstCry, BigBasket and Cars24 = G (inventory-led). MakeMyTrip and ixigo = A (commission; M is the alternative). Rapido = A (mix of commission and driver subscription).
