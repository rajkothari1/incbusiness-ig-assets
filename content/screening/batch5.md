## Notes
- Loss-makers (4): Devyani, Sapphire, Restaurant Brands Asia, United Foodbrands (Barbeque Nation). Westlife, Prataap, Tilaknagar and Sula are only thinly profitable.
- Tilaknagar's reported FY26 PAT (~₹21 Cr) was hit by Imperial Blue acquisition amortisation and exceptional items. Its TTM is a loss of about ₹36 Cr, so "profitable" is fragile. The company's own adjusted PAT is about ₹299 Cr.
- Gopal Snacks' FY26 profit is inflated by insurance claims from the Rajkot plant fire. Operating EBITDA fell.
- Restaurant Brands Asia: Screener shows -204 (total). Stockanalysis and Arihant show -187 (owners' share). Avanti and Indigo also differ between total and owners' share.
- Barbeque Nation renamed itself United Foodbrands Ltd (NSE: UFBL) in Sep 2025. Stockanalysis shows -59 vs -62 on Screener and ScanX.
- Second sources for Bajaj Consumer, Avanti, Globus, Tips, Indigo, Sirca, TTK, Hawkins, Prataap, Westlife, Tilaknagar and Jyothy are data aggregators (Screener plus Stockanalysis), not news articles. The web-search quota ran out partway through the batch.
- Category judgement calls: Mrs Bectors also does B2B buns for QSRs (still mainly E). Globus is mostly bulk alcohol for others (H). Sirca distributes an Italian brand under exclusive tie-up (E, could argue F).
