## Notes
- **Maruti:** the consolidated figure differs by source: 14,619 (IANS) vs 14,679.5 (Outlook Business). The standalone figure is 14,445.
- **Bajaj Auto:** the company's press release confirms 10,744 consolidated. It is boosted by consolidating KTM, including a one-time remeasurement gain of about 1,195. GoodReturns gives 10,574.5, which may be the share attributable to owners.
- **IndiGo:** the reported net loss is 2,394 (Rs 23,936 mn). It came from forex losses and labour-code exceptional items. Excluding those, adjusted profit is about 7,503 (Rs 75,025 mn), so the core business is profitable.
- **Apollo:** the only hits for 1,942 were syndicated copies on unrelated domains. The search budget ran out before a mainstream second source could be found, so confirm this figure on NSE or BSE before using it.
- **Category calls that could go either way:** IRCTC could be A or M (I chose A because convenience fees on ticketing are its main profit driver). Shiprocket could be A or C. Byju's could be B or C. Spinny could be G or A.
- **SUGAR:** FY26 revenue is estimated at about 300–380 (Mint, Exchange4media), but no FY26 profit or loss figure has been published.
