## Notes

- **Reported profit includes one-off items:**
  - HUL: if you want profit from the business itself, use the 10,652 continuing-operations figure.
  - ITC: the FY25 base includes the hotel demerger gain.
  - Wakefit: profit includes a deferred tax gain.
  - PVR INOX: Q4 includes a gain from selling a subsidiary.
  - Patanjali: FY24 profit includes the share-sale gain.
  - Jubilant: the figure includes discontinued operations.
- **Category judgement calls:**
  - PVR INOX is put under M (leisure); K is also arguable.
  - Nazara is put under D.
  - Titan is put under E rather than G because it is a brand owner.
  - Kalyan is put under G (showroom chain).
  - Vedant is put under F because its stores are mostly franchisee-run.
  - Wow! Momo is put under G (owns its outlets), while Rebel Foods is put under E (brands with no stores).
- **Rows where the second source is weaker:**
  - Haldiram's: CRISIL is the only source.
  - Licious: the net loss comes from Inc42 alone.
  - Rebel Foods: the size of the loss cut differs between Inc42 (16.3%) and the company release (19%).
  - Nazara: the second source is a results summary site.
  - Trent: the multibagg article says "profit" without saying it is net profit.
