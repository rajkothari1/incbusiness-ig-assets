## 2. Client preferences (learned from feedback)

1. **Hook in the first 1–3 seconds** with illustrations, so the viewer doesn't scroll away. The stakes/penalty hook was approved.
2. **Livekeeping logo at the very start** (badge from frame 1) **and** a full logo reveal right before the app screens.
3. **Use the real Livekeeping app screenshots**, made interactive: zoom-in/zoom-out, pans, highlights, tap animations.
4. **The manager must look friendly and trustworthy**, never villain-like. **Standing still** when calming the driver, not walking.
5. **Narration must stay energetic for the whole video.** The original ending CTA voice sounded tired and was replaced.
6. **Top text in Hinglish** (e.g. "Truck taiyaar… par E-Way Bill nahi!"), not Devanagari.
7. **Bottom subtitles in English.**
8. They preferred the **longer 33s version** over the tight 25s cut.
9. **Credits:** "use them well." Generate still images first, then animate only the approved ones.

### Audio
- **Music:** custom synth (`source/synth.py`). Tense drone, heartbeat and clock ticks until the app section, then upbeat 120bpm (C–Am–F–G) to the end, with a final hit and no fade.
- **SFX:** hook gong, sub-boom, siren, stamp thud (`source/hook_sfx.py`); whooshes on cuts, riser into the app, logo chime, tap clicks, typing, success ding, truck engine and horn.
- **Mix:** voice lines loudness-matched; music side-chain ducked under voice; limiter at 0.95. Every section measures -17 to -18.5 dB mean.

## 7. Production pipeline

1. **Character sheet** → Higgsfield `nano_banana_pro` (2 credits)
2. **Scene stills** → `nano_banana_pro`, 9:16, 2k, using the character sheet / earlier scene as `image_references` (2 credits each)
3. **Animation** → `kling3_0`, `mode: pro`, `sound: off`, 9:16, 3–5s (≈1.5 credits/sec)
4. **Voices** → `text2speech_v2`, `variant: elevenlabs` (~0.3 credits per line)
5. **App screens, captions, logo sting, end card, hook text** → HTML + JS `render(t)` timeline, rendered frame-by-frame at 30fps with Playwright/Chromium, then ffmpeg
6. **Music & SFX** → synthesized in Python/numpy
7. **Edit** → ffmpeg: concat scenes, PNG/alpha-MOV overlays, voice placement, side-chain mix (`source/build2.sh`, `build3.sh`, `build4.sh`)
