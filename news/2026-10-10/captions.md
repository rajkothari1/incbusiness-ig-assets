# Startup & Tech News Cards: 10 Oct 2026

Instagram portrait cards (1080x1350) made in Canva. Captions and sources for each card are below.

## 1. DailyObjects raises $34.3 Mn Series C
DailyObjects has raised $34.3 Mn (₹332 Cr) in a Series C led by Xponentia Capital, with Anicut Capital and Axiom Asia joining. That makes it the biggest Indian startup round of the week. 📈

#DailyObjects #StartupFunding #D2C #IndianStartups #SeriesC #incbusiness

Sources: Inc42 weekly funding report and StartupTalky daily roundup, 8 Oct 2026.

## 2. Quanfluence raises $10 Mn for room-temperature quantum computers
Bengaluru deeptech startup Quanfluence raised a $10 Mn Series A led by Chiratae Ventures, with Rainmatter by Zerodha and Pi Ventures. Its quantum computers use light and run at room temperature. Next up is a 4-qubit machine, and it is aiming for a 100-qubit prototype by 2029. ⚛️

#Quanfluence #QuantumComputing #DeepTech #Bengaluru #StartupFunding #incbusiness

Sources: StartupTalky, 7 Oct 2026, and Inc42.

## 3. Indian startup funding falls 47% this week
Indian startups raised $122.9 Mn across 25 deals between Oct 5 and Oct 9, down 47.4% from $233.6 Mn the week before. Ecommerce took the biggest share at $63.9 Mn across nine deals. 📉

#StartupFunding #IndianStartups #VentureCapital #FundingRoundup #incbusiness

Source: Inc42 weekly funding report, Oct 5–9, 2026.

## 4. Manus raises $500 Mn+ after Meta deal unwound
Butterfly Effect, the parent company of AI agent startup Manus, has raised more than $500 Mn. Boyu Capital and IDG Capital led the round, with Tencent, HSG and ZhenFund also investing. It comes after China ordered Meta's $2 Bn+ acquisition of Manus to be unwound. 🤖

#Manus #AIAgents #AIFunding #Meta #TechNews #incbusiness

Sources: Reuters (via Mobile World Live and TechNode) and Decrypt, 8 Oct 2026.

## 5. Microsoft and Nvidia launch the $2,599 Surface Laptop Ultra
Microsoft's new 15-inch Surface Laptop Ultra runs on Nvidia's RTX Spark chip and can run AI models with more than 120B parameters on the laptop itself. Prices start at $2,599. Pre-orders are open and sales start Oct 16. 💻

#SurfaceLaptopUltra #Nvidia #Microsoft #AIPC #TechNews #incbusiness

Sources: TechCrunch, TechRepublic and PCWorld, 7–8 Oct 2026.
