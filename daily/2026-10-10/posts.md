## #1 · Earnings

**Headline:** DMart Q2 Profit Rises 8.5% to ₹743 Crore as Revenue Climbs 17.8%

**Paragraph:** DMart's parent Avenue Supermarts earned ₹743 crore in July to September, up 8.5% from ₹685 crore a year ago, on ₹19,644 crore revenue. Profit still fell 13.6% from last quarter as margins slipped, a sign quick commerce is squeezing even India's cheapest supermarket.

## #9 · Mutual Funds · single-sourced

**Headline:** Axis AMC Launches Accelerate SIF for Investors With ₹10 Lakh Minimum

**Paragraph:** Axis Asset Management launched Accelerate SIF, its Specialised Investment Fund arm led by Nandik Mallik. SIFs sit between mutual funds and PMS (portfolio management services) and need a ₹10 lakh minimum. That allows bolder strategies like long short bets. Expect more fund houses to chase wealthier investors.

**Sources:**
- [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/mutual-funds/Axis-AMC-Enters-Specialized-Investment-Funds-With-Accelerate-SIF/6ac8d06879a16deaeaebf223) (single-sourced for the launch)
