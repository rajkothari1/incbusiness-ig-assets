    Good to know

    Still unsure? Install the app and try it free for 7 days.

    How do I connect my Tally to Livekeeping?

    Download the connector on the PC where your Tally runs, sign in with your registered mobile number, select your company and click Sync.

    Does it work with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9?

    Yes. The same connector works with both TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9, with single user as well as multi user options.

    Can I use Livekeeping in a browser?

    Yes. Your synced data is available on the Livekeeping mobile app and on the web, so you can work from your phone or any computer.

    Does my computer need to stay on for syncing?

    Yes. The connector reads data from your Tally account, so the PC should be switched on, connected to the internet, and Tally should be open with your company loaded.

    Can I create invoices, ledgers and items from the app?

    Yes. You can create all types of vouchers through the Livekeeping mobile app, and entries sync with your accounting software.

    Is my Tally data safe?

    Data is sent over an encrypted connection and stored on ISO 27001 certified systems. Livekeeping is ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 certified.

    Can I try it for free?

    Yes. You get a 7-day free trial when you install the Livekeeping mobile app from the Play Store or App Store.
