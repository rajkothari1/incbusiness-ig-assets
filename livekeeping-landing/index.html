    Questions

    Good to know

    Do I need to stop using Tally?

    No. Livekeeping works alongside your existing Tally. You keep working on Tally as usual, and the app mirrors your data to your phone.

    Does the PC need to stay on?

    Your Tally PC syncs data when it's online. Reports you've already synced stay available on your phone even when the PC is off.

    Can I enter data from my phone?

    Yes. Invoices, orders and receipts created in the app sync back to Tally, so there's no double entry.

    Can I manage multiple companies?

    Yes. Link more than one Tally company and switch between them inside the app.

    Is my data safe?

    Data moves over encrypted connections, and you decide which team members see which companies.
