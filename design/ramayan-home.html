Ramayan Comic Chat: Home

The home screen should do one job: get someone reading or chatting within one tap. With 500+ panels it should not show everything. It shows six focused blocks, ordered by what a returning reader wants first.

  1. Continue reading

    A large hero panel from the reader's current episode, with progress (42/80) and a play button. This drives retention more than any other block. New users see "Start with Bal Kand" here instead.

  2. Chat with characters

    Story-style rings for Ram, Sita, Hanuman and others. A coloured ring means a new message or panel, and a grey ring means seen. This is your unique feature, so it sits near the top.

  3. The 7 Kands

    Use the real structure of the Ramayan as the primary navigation: Bal, Ayodhya, Aranya, Kishkindha, Sundar, Yuddha and Uttar. Tall poster cards show episode count and progress.

  4. Life lessons

    Swipeable cards that each pair one lesson with the character and kand it comes from, such as keeping your word (Ram), serving with confidence (Hanuman) and the cost of ego (Ravan). Each card has a value tag and a read time, and opens into the comic panels plus a chat about the lesson.

  5. Explore by theme

    Your 10+ categories go here as a 4-column grid of icon tiles, such as Battles, Bhakti, For kids, Diwali, Shlokas and Quiz, with "12 themes" opening the full list.

  6. Panel of the day

    A daily chaupai in Devanagari with its English meaning. "Ask Ram why" opens the chat, and "Share card" exports an Instagram-ready image. It gives people a reason to open the app daily.

  7. Trending panels

    A 2-column masonry grid of your comic images. Mixed heights suit comic panels and make the 500-image library feel rich. It scrolls endlessly.

  8. Bottom nav with a Chat button

    Home, Explore, Library and Profile, with a raised saffron Chat button in the centre so the core action is always one tap away.
